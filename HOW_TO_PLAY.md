# Last Light: how to play

## Your mission

Rakshasa warriors emerge from the spooky cave and follow the paved road to the small home at the far end. Keep the home’s integrity above zero through 10 levels, each containing five rounds (50 waves total). Your mechanical guns aim and fire automatically. You choose where to build them, which guns to buy, and when to strengthen the home.

The map uses a fixed 45-degree view so the cave, the road's broad snake-like bends, the defenses, and the destination house remain in view together. The road connects opposite sides of the battlefield without crossing itself. From level 3, enemies use two parallel lines. From level 5 onward they use three lines. The whole road has shoulders, curbs, road paint, and smooth bends.

## First launch

A welcome card appears the first time. Choose **Take the guided demo** for the click-by-click walkthrough, or **Start playing** to begin. The guide highlights one control, explains what it does, and waits for you to use it. You can restart the guide from **GUIDE DEMO** at the top. The demo uses a practice campaign and resets it when it starts.

## Start a campaign

1. **Prepare before the first wave.** You start with 330 credits and the Sentinel gun. Open **Weapon Shop** to unlock a gun, reinforce the home, or build support houses. The shop is only available between rounds. During the 1.5-second round report, opening the shop pauses the countdown.
2. **Buy and place a new gun.** The Shop shows the combined total for its blueprint and first turret. When you can afford it, click **BUY**; the gun is added to **Defense Systems** and placement mode starts. Click open ground beside the road to place it. Later copies cost only the build price shown on the gun card. Each gun type can be placed up to three times; the shop shows **STOCK COMPLETE** after the third copy.
3. **Place it beside the road.** Choose a Defense Systems card, then click open ground near a bend or straight stretch. Roadside placement is rejected if it overlaps the road or another turret base. Firing ranges may overlap. Press Escape to cancel placement.
4. **Start level 1.** Click **BEGIN DEFENSE**. The first level starts when you choose; after each cleared level, a report appears for 1.5 seconds and the next level starts automatically.
5. **Let the guns defend.** Turrets find targets and fire without further clicks. Earn credits automatically for every enemy defeated. Clearing a level also pays a bonus.
6. **Buy and improve between levels.** Click **Weapon Shop** during a break to add more guns or protect your home. Click a placed turret to select it, then use **Upgrade** or **Sell** beneath the battlefield.
7. **Survive to level 10 (50 rounds total).** Later levels bring more durable and more numerous enemies. Levels 5 through 10 end with a boss round. The left mission panel remembers the highest level reached in this browser.

## The six guns

Each gun has a one-time blueprint unlock price and a separate price for each turret placed. Higher priced guns hit harder or cover a wider area, and reload more slowly. Lower priced guns are easier to afford and fire faster or at a normal pace. You can upgrade each deployed gun twice, up to level 3: upgrades increase damage and range, and shorten its reload time a little. Higher levels cost more.

| Gun | Blueprint | Build | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Sentinel** | Available at start | 90 CR | Balanced, affordable starter gun |
| **Gatling** | 120 CR | 175 CR | Fast repeated shots at close range |
| **Cryo** | 175 CR | 250 CR | Slows enemies so other guns can attack longer |
| **Railgun** | 350 CR | 480 CR | Heavy single hits and long reach, with slower fire |
| **Mortar** | 500 CR | 650 CR | Powerful splash damage over a broad area |
| **Cataclysm** | 720 CR | 880 CR | Highest damage and widest reach; slowest volleys |

Unlocks are remembered for the current campaign and return to the starter gun when you restart. A turret you already own can be upgraded at any time outside an active wave. Selling a turret refunds part of its cost.

## Home and support houses

The small house at the road’s end is the objective. Enemies that reach it damage its integrity. Reinforce it in the shop up to level 5. Each reinforcement adds 10 maximum integrity and repairs 10 immediately. Higher levels reduce some breach damage and repair more integrity after each cleared level.

You can also build up to two of each settlement support house:

- **Watch House — 260 CR:** repairs 2 home integrity after each cleared level.
- **Supply House — 300 CR:** adds 25 credits to every level-clear reward.

These buildings appear around the destination house and change the strategy by improving recovery or income.

## Controls and screen

- **Weapon Shop:** unlock and prepare the first gun placement, reinforce the home, or build support houses between rounds. It is unavailable while enemies are attacking.
- **Defense Systems cards:** select a gun type and enter placement mode for another turret. The number keys 1–5 also select the first five owned guns. Selecting a card always prepares that gun for placement. Press Escape to cancel.
- **Battlefield click:** place the selected gun on valid open ground, or select a built gun to show its firing range and actions.
- **Upgrade:** strengthen the selected turret. Each new level increases shot damage and range while slightly increasing firing speed.
- **Sell:** remove the selected turret and recover some credits.
- **BEGIN DEFENSE:** starts the first level. Later levels start on their own after the completion report.
- **Pause / Resume:** freeze or continue the battle. Press **Space** to toggle pause.
- **Speed:** cycle between 1×, 1.5×, and 2× simulation speed.
- **Restart arrow:** clear the current battle and restore home integrity while preserving your credits, guns, upgrades, and settlement progress. The best level reached remains saved in this browser.
- **End of game:** after a win or defeat, choose **END GAME** or **RESTART GAME**. Both clear the current battle and restore home integrity only; credits, guns, upgrades, house improvements, score, and kills remain available.
- **Performance Stress Test:** replace the campaign with a fixed performance fixture. Restart to return to a fresh game.

The top cards show home integrity, credits, defeated enemies, and score. The wave badge shows campaign progress. The battlefield status and telemetry show active enemy, tower, and projectile counts plus a rolling FPS estimate.

## Enemies

- **Rakshasa Scout:** quick, lightly armored, and deals 1 home damage.
- **Rakshasa Brute:** slow and heavily armored; deals 3 home damage.
- **Rakshasa Runner:** very fast and fragile; deals 1 home damage.
- **Shield-bearer:** durable and steady; deals 2 home damage.

Their colors and silhouettes distinguish them on the road. A thin green line above each enemy shows remaining health and shortens as guns damage it; no number is displayed. From level 3 they arrive in two side-by-side lanes. At level 5 and above they arrive in three. Enemies follow the same winding road between the cave and the house.

## Suggested strategy

Start with Sentinel guns around early bends. Spend credits earned from kills and level clears on wider coverage, faster fire, and home reinforcement. Add Gatlings where the road passes close. Use Cryo near another turret so slowed enemies stay in range longer. Railguns cover long stretches; Mortars and Cataclysm artillery can damage groups, with artillery reaching farthest and hitting hardest. From level 7, invest in a higher-power gun and upgrades before the tougher formations overwhelm the starter layout. A Supply House pays back over time; a Watch House helps recover from leaks.

## End states

- **Victory:** clear all five rounds in level 10 while the house still stands.
- **Game over:** home integrity reaches zero. Use **Deploy Again** or the restart arrow to begin a new campaign.

## What has and has not been verified

The game includes 10 levels and 50 rounds, six gun types, four enemy types, automatic attacks, placement and upgrades, selling, the house shop, score, victory/game-over states, pause, restart, speed, and an active stress fixture with 5,000 enemies, 100 guns, and 1,000 projectiles. In the local browser sample before the final road waypoint revision, the warmed-up 300-frame reading showed 56 FPS, with 4.3% of frames slower than 45 FPS and 4.3% over 33 ms. These numbers depend on the browser and device; retest the final route shape for an exact reading. Accelerated simulation reached the 50th-round victory state; a complete browser heap/memory profile has not been run, so long-run memory stability remains unverified. See `README.md` and `NUMBERS.md` for the design and measurement details.
